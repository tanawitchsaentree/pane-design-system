Merging · two lenses on one surface

Drag the right lens toward the left one. Their outline is a smooth union of the two, so a neck forms before they touch and the bend follows it.

How it is drawn. A canvas computes the signed distance d to the shape, rounded corners and all. Near the edge (within the band b), the map pushes each sample inward along the edge normal by A·(1 − |d|/b)³, which is the bend. Over the whole face it pulls samples toward the centre by k·r, so the middle magnifies a little like a lens. A uses three maps with slightly different strengths for red, green and blue, so a coloured edge separates the way real glass disperses. The result is a backdrop-filter: url(#…) filter. Text stays on the flat middle, where the bend is near zero, so its contrast is measured as before.
